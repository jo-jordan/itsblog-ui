This blog grew up looking like a **Mac OS X** computer. Here is how to find your way around.

## Reading posts

- Click **Finder** in the Dock, or double-click **Macintosh HD** in the top right corner of the desktop. Categories are on the left, posts in the middle and a preview on the right.
- **Double-click** a post (or click “Open” above the preview) to open it in a window of its own. The link in the address bar can then be shared with anyone.
- Looking for a particular post? Use **Sherlock** in the Dock; results are sorted by relevance.

## Windows

- Drag the title bar to move a window, and its bottom right corner to resize it.
- The three buttons at the top left: red closes, yellow minimizes into the Dock, green zooms. Hover over them to see × − +.
- Double-clicking the title bar minimizes as well.

## Other bits and pieces

- Click the apple at the top left for **About This Mac** and **System Preferences** (change the desktop picture, adjust the Dock, switch to the Graphite appearance, or pick a language under International). You can also put the computer to sleep or shut it down.
- **Mail** writes straight to the site's owner, and **Address Book** has the contact details.

## How this site is made

Posts are Markdown files kept in the repository, bundled into the site at build time and hosted on Cloudflare. Each post begins with a little metadata:
